# Digital Detox — Support

Digital Detox locks the apps you choose until you earn screen time back with real
effort: push-ups, squats, jumping jacks, walking or focused work.

**Requires iOS 18 or later, on iPhone.**

## Get help

Email **[dhruwangjariwala18@gmail.com](mailto:dhruwangjariwala18@gmail.com)**, or
tap **Account → Send feedback** in the app. Include your iOS version and what you
were doing when it went wrong — that is usually enough to identify the problem on
the first reply.

## Common problems

### My apps are not being blocked

Digital Detox blocks apps through Screen Time, and iOS will let the app run
without that permission — silently, with nothing blocked.

1. Open Digital Detox. If the home screen says **"Screen Time access is off"**,
   tap **Fix this in Settings** and grant access. You can also check
   **Account → Screen Time access**.
2. Otherwise check **Settings → Screen Time** is on and has not been turned off
   by a device management profile or a parent/guardian account.
3. Confirm you have actually picked apps: **Account → Locked apps**. Digital
   Detox cannot pick for you — iOS never tells the app which apps exist, so the
   selection has to be yours.

### Walking is not earning me anything

Walking is credited from the step count your iPhone keeps itself, so Digital
Detox needs **Motion & Fitness** access.

1. **Settings → Privacy & Security → Motion & Fitness** — make sure Fitness
   Tracking is on and **Digital Detox** is allowed.
2. If the walking card says Digital Detox can't see your steps, tap **Open
   Settings** on the card and allow Motion & Fitness there.
3. Walking pays per whole 1,000 steps (10 minutes each). Anything under that is
   shown as "N steps to your next +10 min" — it is not lost.

Steps are counted by the iPhone you carry. Steps recorded only by an Apple Watch
do not count, and nothing about your steps leaves your phone.

### The camera is not counting my reps

Reps are counted by on-device pose detection, which needs to see you.

1. Follow the placement screen: phone upright against a wall, screen facing you.
2. For push-ups, get about 2 feet away with head and shoulders in frame. For
   squats and jumping jacks, step back until your whole body fits.
3. Even, front-on light helps. Backlighting — a bright window behind you — is the
   usual cause of missed reps.
4. Check **Settings → Digital Detox → Camera** is allowed.

No video is recorded or uploaded. Frames are analysed and discarded.

### My apps locked again before the time was up

Two things can cause this:

- **You locked them early.** Tapping *Lock my apps now* returns the unused
  minutes to your balance on purpose.
- **iOS re-locked slightly late or early.** The system's usage accounting is not
  instant and can be off by up to about a minute. This is why the shortest
  unlock is 15 minutes — at shorter windows the error would be a large share of
  the window.

### The minimum unlock is 15 minutes — can it be shorter?

No. iOS refuses to schedule the underlying monitoring window below 15 minutes,
and shorter unlocks could not be delivered accurately.

### I can't earn any more minutes

Your balance is capped at 240 minutes so a day's earnings cannot be banked
indefinitely and defeat the point. You can set a lower cap yourself under
**Account → Minute cap**. When you are at the cap, spend some before earning
more.

### I paid, but Digital Detox is asking me to buy again

Tap **Restore purchase** — it is on the purchase screen and at the bottom of
**Account**. This asks the App Store to re-sync your purchases. Make sure you are
signed into the same Apple Account you bought with. Digital Detox is a single
one-time purchase, not a subscription — there is nothing to renew and nothing to
cancel.

### My free trial ended sooner than I expected

The 7-day trial starts when you first lock your apps, and its start date is
stored in your iCloud so it survives deleting and reinstalling the app.
Reinstalling does not give you a fresh 7 days.

### Can I change the language?

Yes — **Account → Language**. Digital Detox ships in 16 languages and this
setting is independent of your phone's language.

### How do I stop using Digital Detox entirely?

Clear your selection under **Account → Locked apps**, or revoke access in
**Settings → Screen Time**, then delete the app. Deleting the app alone does not
revoke Screen Time authorisation, because iOS keeps that at the system level.

## Privacy

Digital Detox has no account, no advertising and no server of its own. It sends
a small stream of anonymous usage events — which screens were reached, whether a
purchase worked — with no identifier from your device. The apps you block, your
steps, your camera and your habit history never leave your phone. Full details
are in the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md).
